The level where Polish stops being a phrasebook. Four more cases arrive, each
introduced by what it does rather than as a table to memorise; the past tense
brings verbal aspect out into the open; and the motion verbs begin their long
career. Goal: the A2 model exam.

## What the level covers

{{< card-grid >}}
{{< card >}}
**Topics**
Shopping and quantities; the doctor and the body; travel, tickets and
timetables; the flat and household matters; work and study routines; past
events and holidays; making arrangements and cancelling them.
{{< /card >}}
{{< card >}}
**Grammar**
The genitive, dative, instrumental and locative in their commonest functions;
the past tense with gender agreement; aspect pairs named and drilled; the
determinate/indeterminate motion verbs (*iść/chodzić*, *jechać/jeździć*);
plural forms, including the virile/non-virile split.
{{< /card >}}
{{< card >}}
**Aspect**
The imperfective/perfective distinction is now explicit. It is taught as a
question about the speaker's view of an event — is it a process or a completed
whole — not as a rule about time.
{{< /card >}}
{{< card >}}
**Register**
The *pan/pani* forms extend from greetings into requests, apologies and
complaints, where the choice starts to carry real social weight.
{{< /card >}}
{{< /card-grid >}}

## The grammar thread

| Case | Introduced through | Typical A2 use |
|---|---|---|
| Genitive | Absence and quantity | *nie ma…*, *nie mam…*, after quantity words, dates, possession, and the commonest prepositions (*do*, *od*, *z*, *bez*, *dla*) |
| Locative | Location | Only after prepositions — *w*, *na*, *o*, *przy*, *po* — which makes it the easiest case to bound and a good one to meet early |
| Instrumental | Identity and accompaniment | *jestem…* generalised from its A1 frame; *z* + person; means of transport |
| Dative | The recipient | *daję komuś…*, *pomagam…*, *podoba mi się…*, and the impersonal *jest mi zimno* pattern |

The vocative is met in address formulas at this level — *panie doktorze*,
*Kasiu* — as a form to recognise and reproduce, and is treated systematically
at [B1](/level-b1/).

## The exam

An **A2 model exam** closes the level: reading, listening, writing and
speaking, with a printable PDF, a stated duration, a point total and a pass
mark. Like every exam here it is the author's own adaptation and confers
nothing.
