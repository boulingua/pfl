At B1 the pieces are assembled. The seven cases are consolidated into a system
you can navigate rather than a set of endings you recall one at a time; aspect
moves from single sentences into extended narration; and the numeral rules that
learners routinely dodge are met head-on. With B1 complete, the course meets
its declared <code>core</code> conformance target.

## What the level covers

{{< card-grid >}}
{{< card >}}
**Topics**
Work and study; housing and bureaucracy; media and current events; health and
the body at greater length; opinions, agreement and disagreement; recounting
what happened and what someone else said.
{{< /card >}}
{{< card >}}
**Grammar**
The full case system consolidated, including the vocative treated
systematically; aspect in narration and in the future; the conditional; verbal
government (which verb takes which case); comparatives and superlatives;
numeral agreement in full.
{{< /card >}}
{{< card >}}
**Numeral government**
The 2–4 versus 5-and-above split, the genitive of quantity, and the way a
numeral phrase governs its verb. This is deliberately placed here rather than
in an early unit, where it would swamp everything around it.
{{< /card >}}
{{< card >}}
**Mediation**
The first mediation tasks proper: relaying the content of a text or an
announcement to someone who has not read or heard it, and explaining a term to
a person who does not have it.
{{< /card >}}
{{< /card-grid >}}

## Consolidation, not repetition

The B1 case work is not a second pass over A2. It addresses the three things
that a function-first sequence leaves outstanding:

1. **Competition between cases.** Where two cases are both grammatical and mean
   different things — the genitive versus accusative object under negation, the
   accusative versus locative after *w* and *na* — the choice is the lesson.
2. **Verbal government.** Which case a verb demands is a lexical fact that has
   to be learned with the verb; B1 is where the habit of learning it that way is
   established.
3. **The plural in full.** The virile/non-virile split reaches beyond the
   nominative into the whole paradigm, and it interacts with numeral agreement.

## Conformance

With B1 complete the course satisfies its declared **`core`** conformance
target: every in-scope scale of the
[boulingua curriculum framework](https://github.com/boulingua/curriculum) that
has an official A1, A2 or B1 descriptor is implemented, and cells the CEFR
Companion Volume leaves empty are recorded as `no-official-descriptor`. The
mediation and plurilingual scales are the thinnest part of that coverage and
are declared partial where that is the accurate description — see
[About](/about/).

## The exam
