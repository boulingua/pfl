{{< kicker >}}LEVEL B2 · UPPER INTERMEDIATE · PARTIAL EXTENSION{{< /kicker >}}
{{< lead >}}
The level at which Polish becomes a working language rather than a studied one:
abstract subjects, an argument sustained over several turns, the difference
between how a thing is said in an office and how it is said among friends, and
the participial constructions that written Polish leans on and spoken Polish
mostly avoids.
{{< /lead >}}
{{< /hero >}}

## What the level covers

{{< card-grid >}}
{{< card >}}
**Topics**
Work and professional correspondence; education and qualifications; society,
environment and technology; culture and the press; argument and negotiation;
complaint and redress.
{{< /card >}}
{{< card >}}
**Grammar**
Adjectival and adverbial participles; the passive and its alternatives;
reported speech; verbal nouns; word order as an information-structuring device;
aspect in the subtler cases, including habitual and iterative readings.
{{< /card >}}
{{< card >}}
**Register**
Explicit work on register: administrative Polish, journalistic Polish and
colloquial Polish as three describable varieties a learner has to move between,
rather than as good and bad usage.
{{< /card >}}
{{< card >}}
**Mediation**
Extended mediation: summarising a longer text for a specific reader,
reconciling two accounts, and explaining a Polish concept to someone without
the cultural background for it.
{{< /card >}}
{{< /card-grid >}}

## A declared partial extension

B2 sits **beyond** the course's declared `core` conformance target, which spans
A1–B1. It is not declared as `full` conformance, because the framework's
`full` level also mandates C1, which this edition does not attempt. Calling B2
a *partial extension* is the honest posture the
[curriculum framework](https://github.com/boulingua/curriculum) expects:
declare the gap rather than redefine the standard.

The mediation and plurilingual scales remain the thinnest part of the coverage
at this level. Open educational material for mediation is scarce in any
language and scarcer in Polish; what can genuinely be supported is implemented,
and the rest is marked as such in the conformance manifest.

C1 and C2 are out of scope for this edition. Freely available Polish material
rarely reaches C1 at a usable standard, and attempting it here would produce
coverage claims the content could not support.

## The exam

A **B2 model exam** closes the level, in the same shape as the others.
