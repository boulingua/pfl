Every unit carries a slide deck and a worksheet as PDFs, generated locally from
the shared branded `slidegen` and `sheetgen` LaTeX templates and committed to
the repository, so that what you download is exactly what was built and
reviewed. Continuous integration verifies that the files are present and
correctly attributed; it does not regenerate them.

All materials are licensed **CC BY-SA 4.0** and carry their attribution on the
page. Print them, cut them up, translate them, put them in a coursepack — the
only condition is that the attribution travels with them and that derivatives
carry the same licence.

{{< callout type="note" >}}
**Polish diacritics in print.** The LaTeX toolchain is pinned to a font with
full Latin Extended-A coverage, so that ł, the ogonek on ą and ę, and the acute
accents on ć ń ó ś ź render correctly rather than falling back to a substitute
glyph. A missing diacritic in a printed worksheet is a defect, not a cosmetic
issue.
{{< /callout >}}

Audio lives with its unit rather than here: each unit page has a **Listen**
section with one player per segment and the full transcript underneath.
