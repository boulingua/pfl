One worksheet per unit, built from the shared branded `sheetgen` template, with
an answer key. Worksheets are laid out for A4 printing in black and white:
tables and gap-fill exercises stay legible on a photocopier, and the accent
colour is used only where its loss costs nothing.

Worksheets appear here as the units they belong to are written.
